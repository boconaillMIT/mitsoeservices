Assign access

Limited access covers materials approved for junior staff. Unit-wide access also includes the Full access page and wallets. Write roles allow viewing and editing where editing is enabled. Unit administrators may assign all five levels within their own unit.

School-wide grants apply to every unit. RCT and Donation Requests each require their own grant; school administrators retain RCT management access. RCT grants never provide access to school or unit finances. A unit grant does not remove access inherited from a school-wide grant.

Department-only budget viewers: choose an area beginning “Budget viewer”, then save one read-only permission for each assigned department. This does not grant RCT, unit-finance or editing access. Existing school-wide grants still apply; remove those if the person must see only selected department budgets.
